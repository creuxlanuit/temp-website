CREUX LA NUIT

Creux La Nuit is a French House and electronic adjacent record label that started out as a collective in late 2023. Founded by Opierm, helping fellow artists widen their reach and their platforms through Discord, amassed a community of around 200 members alongside 20 releasing artists. Gaining notoriety from fellow labels and artists such as OMEAC Records, Montaime Records, Todd Edwards and more, Creux La Nuit continues to expand their palate, releasing their fourth instalment in their compilation series in mid 2026.
